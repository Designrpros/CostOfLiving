🎒 Backpack & Packing Guide

The ultimate packing list for your European adventure

🎒 Backpack Essentials

Clothing

Item Notes
🧥 Light jacket Layers for changing weather
👕 T-shirts 3-4 breathable tops
👖 Pants/Shorts 2 pants, 1-2 shorts
🧦 Socks 4-5 pairs
🩳 Underwear 5-6 pairs
🧢 Hat Sun protection
🧣 Scarf For colder days

Footwear

Item Notes
👟 Walking shoes Comfortable for cities
🩴 Flip flops Hostel/Beach
👢 Waterproof shoes Rainy seasons

Toiletries

Item Notes
🪥 Toothbrush & Paste
🧴 Shampoo/Soap Travel size
☀️ Sunscreen Essential summer
💊 First Aid Kit Basic supplies
🧻 Quick-dry towel Lightweight

Tech

Item Notes
📱 Phone With offline maps
🔌 Power adapter Most of Europe: Type C/F. UK, Ireland, Malta, Cyprus: Type G. Switzerland: Type J. Italy also uses Type L
🔋 Power bank For long days
🎧 Headphones Noise cancelling
📷 Camera Optional

Documents

Item Notes
🛂 Passport From outside the EU: valid at least 3 months after you leave the Schengen area, and issued in the last 10 years
💳 Bank cards Notify bank abroad
🏥 Travel insurance Essential!
🏨 Booking confirmations Print/digital
📄 Emergency contacts

Entering Europe from outside the EU

  • Entry/Exit System (EES): since April 2026, travellers from outside the EU have their fingerprints and a photo taken at the Schengen border the first time, in place of passport stamps. Allow extra time at the border. (European Commission)
  • ETIAS: a €20 travel authorisation for visa-free visitors is coming but not yet in operation; the EU will announce the start date months ahead. Do not pay sites that claim to sell it now. (Official ETIAS site)

💡 Pro Tips

Backpack Size

  • 40-50L - Perfect for weekend trips
  • 50-65L - Extended trips (1-3 months)
  • Carry-on - Limits vary by airline; budget airlines often include only a small under-seat bag

Weight Tips

  • Pack light! Max 10kg recommended
  • Roll clothes to save space
  • Wear heaviest items on plane

Money Saving

  • Bring reusable water bottle
  • Buy food at supermarkets
  • Use local SIM or eSIM

🌧️ Seasonal Packing

Summer (Jun-Aug)

  • Light breathable clothing
  • Sunscreen
  • Sunglasses
  • Small daypack

Winter (Dec-Feb)

  • Warm layers
  • Thermal underwear
  • Good waterproof jacket
  • Warm boots

Shoulder (Mar-May, Sep-Nov)

  • Mix of both
  • Always bring a jacket
  • Layers are key
