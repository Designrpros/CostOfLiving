# 💰 Affiliate links

Everything on costofliving.no is free to read, and the prices come from open data: Eurostat, the World Bank, Statistics Norway and the European Central Bank. Almost every link on the site is a plain link that earns us nothing.

## The links that can earn us something

| Service | What it is | What we may get |
|---------|------------|-----------------|
| [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/c/alcatel5) | Private homes and rooms | A referral reward, if you sign up through the link |
| [Revolut](https://revolut.com/referral/?referral-code=alcatel) | Bank account and card | A referral reward, if you open an account through the link |

Using them costs you nothing extra.

## How you can tell

Every link that can earn us money is marked as sponsored in the page's code, and any page or section that has one says so beside it. If we add more, they will be listed here and marked the same way.
