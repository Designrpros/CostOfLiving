🇳🇴Cost of living in Norway

+29% Everyday spending in Norway costs 29% more than the EU average.

Alcohol & tobacco, health and phone & internet cost the most compared with the rest of Europe; housing & utilities, furniture & household and clothing & shoes the least. Rents run from about €710 a month for one room in Bergen to €1,810 for three rooms in Oslo.
